However, before this promising study can be accepted for publication, several revisions are required. These modifications will help enhance the clarity, rigor, and overall quality of the manuscript. Below are specific suggestions for revision.In several instances throughout the manuscript, the authors use the term effect, such as in the objective stated in the abstract: "The current study aimed to explore the mediating effect of vitamin D levels in the link between DI and frailty."
