Conducted with 933 participants aged 60 and above, the study utilized the Rowland Universal Dementia Assessment Scale (RUDAS) to measure cognitive impairment. The findings revealed that 53.7% of participants had cognitive impairment or were at risk of dementia, with higher rates in women (56.5%) compared to men (51.7%). The prevalence of cognitive impairment increased with age, and significant geographic variations were observed, with higher prevalence rates in the mountainous (67.3%) and hilly (46.2%) regions compared to the low-lying areas (22.9%).While the study provides valuable data on dementia prevalence in Nepal, it has several limitations.
