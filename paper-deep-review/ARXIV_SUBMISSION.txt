1. TITLE (Initial Caps, No LaTeX Syntax):
Self-Evolving Clinical Deep Review: Eliminating Recurring Methodological False Alarms without Compromising Objective Error Recall via Comment-Driven Dual-Track Memory

2. AUTHORS (Full Names Separated by Commas, NO "and", No LaTeX Syntax):
Hui Zhao, Dong Qian, Heurion Research Team

3. ABSTRACT (Single Continuous Paragraph, < 1,920 Chars, < 250 Words, NO Newlines, NO LaTeX Commands):
Automated clinical manuscript review faces a precision-recall-memory trilemma: text-only LLMs miss 87.5% of 2D table arithmetic errors with severe alert fatigue; deterministic checkers miss semantic spin (p>=0.05 framed as efficacy); and globally injecting memorized rebuttals leaks study-specific terms or depresses unseen error sensitivity. We present Heurion Deep Review, a four-stage verification architecture with a Comment-Driven Dual-Track Self-Evolution Loop. It integrates a section-role map and RxC table-grid scratchpad (Stage 1), density routing (Stage 2), four specialist passes across seven error categories (E1-E7) with dual-quote-verified section LLM review (Stage 3), and candidate-scoped full-text verification with cell-level CRDT anchoring (Stage 4). From clinician replies, Track A distills methodological rebuttals into zero-digit, specialty-sanitized boundary rules routed selectively per Stage 4 candidate behind a five-invariant safety floor (F_hard); Track B crystallizes accepted fixes into pre-write guards and additive checklists. Across 19 full-length 2025 PMC papers (57 injected errors), zero-LLM AST achieves 66.7% recall at 88.3% precision (84.8% injected-only) and uncovers 7 published errors. Hybrid review reaches 87.7% recall (50/57, p=0.0018), cuts false positives by 92.5% vs. text-only PAT, outperforms full-context DeepSeek-V3 (+18.7 pp injected precision, p=0.0026), and matches 64k-CoT DeepSeek-R1 F1 (71.0% vs. 71.9%) at 14.4x fewer output tokens. Selective routing lifts evolved recall to 89.5% (83.3% unseen test), eliminating the -12.3 pp 5-fold CV recall loss of global prompt stuffing (p=0.0156). Hardened F_hard blocks 100% (64/64) of adversarial false exemptions, PMC <table-wrap> parsing lifts citation accuracy from 52.8% to 94.4%, and Track B guards raise clean revision pass rates from 58.3% to 87.5%.
