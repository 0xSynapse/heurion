3. ABSTRACT (Single Continuous Paragraph, NO Newlines, NO Extra Whitespace, NO LaTeX Commands):
Automated peer review and scientific verification systems face a fundamental precision-recall dilemma in clinical medicine. On one hand, single-pass large language model (LLM) reviewers and text-only multi-agent pipelines frequently miss objective numerical contradictions between manuscript prose and multi-column tables, inverted or out-of-bounds 95% confidence intervals, and intra-table row/cell arithmetic errors. Conversely, deterministic structural auditors alone struggle to capture open-ended semantic spin bias (p >= 0.05 framed as clinical efficacy), while hybrid AST+LLM pipelines trigger recurring false alarms on legitimate study-design denominator transitions, such as intention-to-treat (ITT) randomized sample sizes versus per-protocol completers in CONSORT trials, pre- versus post-propensity-matching cohorts in STROBE observational studies, or dual parametric/non-parametric reporting across skewed subscales. Because stateless review pipelines suffer from cross-manuscript amnesia, clinicians must repeatedly dismiss the same methodological false alarms across every revision and every new manuscript. We present Heurion Deep Review, a clean-room four-stage clinical manuscript verification architecture paired with a Comment-Driven Dual-Track Self-Evolution Loop (evolveFromReviewCommentAsync). First, our four-stage pipeline combines hierarchical section-role segmentation and a ProseMirror 2D table-grid scratchpad (Stage 1), quantitative-density budget routing (Stage 2), four specialist auditing passes covering seven objective clinical error categories (E1-E7) across external literature, internal prose-table arithmetic, statistical method-result pairing, and spin bias alongside quote-verified LLM section review (Stage 3), and full-text LLM verification with cell-level CRDT comment anchoring {#c:id} (Stage 4). Second, evolveFromReviewCommentAsync learns directly from clinician interactions on anchored comments: (i) when a clinician rebuts a study-design boundary alert (acceptedFix = false), Track A (narrow) uses zero-template LLM rule distillation guarded by dual instance-decoupling checks (hasInstanceLeakage and sensitiveReason) to synthesize a zero-digit reusable boundary rule, while a hard safety floor (NON_EXEMPTABLE_SUBTYPES) guarantees that objective mathematical, CI, and table-sum errors can never be exempted even under adversarial rebuttal; and (ii) Track B (procedural and review_audit checklists) crystallizes corroborated true-positive fixes into pre-write rules and dev-only cross-section audit skills. We evaluate our framework across two benchmarks: (1) a Full-Text Controlled Error-Injection Benchmark of 19 open-access 2025 PubMed Central (PMC) JATS XML manuscripts (38 full-length clean/injected variants averaging ~6,000 words, 57 injected errors across E1-E7, partitioned into 11 dev and 8 held-out test papers) coupled with a physically blind-isolated LLM Simulated Clinician Agent (simulateBlindClinician), and (2) a 7-Vignette Clinical Oncology Benchmark with live deepseek-chat calls across 8 verified organic publication errors. Without a single LLM call or study-specific regex, our clean-room deterministic AST engine alone detects 38/57 (66.7%) of injected errors across full-length PMC manuscripts (80.0% on E1 sample flow, 83.3% on E2 inline arithmetic, 75.0% on E4 CI bounds, and 87.5% on E7 table sums) at 85.5% precision (0.26 true false positives per clean paper, 0.0% false exemption under adversarial rebuttal), while uncovering 7 genuine unannotated in-print errors in published 2025 clinical articles. On the 7-vignette live deepseek-chat oncology benchmark, heurion_evolved lifts organic error recall from 37.5% (3/8, single-pass and PAT text-only baselines) to 100.0% (8/8) while eliminating all 16 recurring clinical boundary false alarms (100.0% F1, 0.0% instance leakage).
