3. ABSTRACT (Single Continuous Paragraph, NO Newlines, NO Extra Whitespace, NO LaTeX Commands):
Automated peer review and scientific verification systems face a fundamental precision-recall dilemma in clinical medicine. On one hand, single-pass large language model (LLM) reviewers and text-only multi-agent pipelines frequently miss objective numerical contradictions between manuscript prose and multi-column tables, inverted or out-of-bounds 95% confidence intervals, and intra-table row/cell arithmetic errors. Conversely, deterministic structural auditors alone struggle to capture open-ended semantic spin bias (p >= 0.05 framed as clinical efficacy), while hybrid AST+LLM pipelines trigger recurring false alarms on legitimate study-design denominator transitions, such as intention-to-treat (ITT) randomized sample sizes versus per-protocol completers in CONSORT trials, pre- versus post-propensity-matching cohorts in STROBE observational studies, or dual parametric/non-parametric reporting across skewed subscales. Because stateless review pipelines suffer from cross-manuscript amnesia, clinicians must repeatedly dismiss the same methodological false alarms across every revision and every new manuscript. We present Heurion Deep Review, a clean-room four-stage clinical manuscript verification architecture paired with a Comment-Driven Dual-Track Self-Evolution Loop (evolveFromReviewCommentAsync). First, our four-stage pipeline combines hierarchical section-role segmentation and a ProseMirror 2D table-grid scratchpad (Stage 1), quantitative-density budget routing (Stage 2), four specialist auditing passes covering seven objective clinical error categories (E1-E7) across external literature, internal prose-table arithmetic, statistical method-result pairing, and spin bias alongside quote-verified LLM section review (Stage 3), and full-text LLM verification with cell-level CRDT comment anchoring {#c:id} (Stage 4). Second, evolveFromReviewCommentAsync learns directly from clinician interactions on anchored comments: (i) when a clinician rebuts a study-design boundary alert (acceptedFix = false), Track A (narrow) uses zero-template LLM rule distillation guarded by dual instance-decoupling checks (hasInstanceLeakage and sensitiveReason) to synthesize a zero-digit reusable boundary rule, while a hard safety floor (NON_EXEMPTABLE_SUBTYPES) guarantees that objective mathematical, CI, and table-sum errors can never be exempted even under adversarial rebuttal; and (ii) Track B (procedural and review_audit checklists) crystallizes corroborated true-positive fixes into pre-write rules and dev-only cross-section audit skills. We evaluate our framework across two benchmarks: (1) a Full-Text Controlled Error-Injection Benchmark of 19 open-access 2025 PubMed Central (PMC) JATS XML manuscripts (38 full-length clean/injected variants averaging ~6,000 words, 57 injected errors across E1-E7, partitioned into 11 dev and 8 held-out test papers) evaluated with both deterministic clean-room AST and live deepseek-v4-flash (3,285 API calls, 7.90M input tokens) coupled with a physically blind-isolated LLM Simulated Clinician Agent (simulateBlindClinician), and (2) a 7-Vignette Clinical Oncology Benchmark with live deepseek-chat calls across 8 verified organic publication errors. Without a single LLM call or study-specific regex, our clean-room AST engine alone detects 38/57 (66.7%) of injected errors at 85.5% precision (0.26 true false positives per clean paper, 0.0% false exemption under adversarial rebuttal) and uncovers 7 genuine in-print errors in published 2025 journals. Adding live deepseek-v4-flash hybrid review (heurion_deep) lifts full-text recall to 48/57 (84.2%, including 3.5x higher E6 spin recall at 77.8% and 25 organic in-print errors) while slashing false positives by 93.0% versus text-only pat_baseline (76 vs. 1,081 FPs). Under physically blind clinician interaction (95.2% [179/188] true-error acceptance and 38.8% [40/103] false-alarm rebuttal), heurion_evolved cuts intra-manuscript Round 2 clean false positives by 34.0% (47 to 31; overall FPs -26.3%, F1 70.2% to 72.8%) and cross-manuscript false positives by 28.9% (76 to 54, F1 70.2% to 74.0%) with zero recall loss (48/57 = 84.2%, achieving 100.0% on E2, E4, and E7). On strictly frozen unseen test papers (N=8), transfer achieves a Pareto improvement, simultaneously lifting test recall from 75.0% to 79.2% (18/24 to 19/24) while cutting clean false positives by 15.8% (19 to 16, F1 63.1% to 64.8%) with 0.0% instance leakage; on the 7-vignette oncology benchmark, heurion_evolved reaches 100.0% recall (8/8) and 100.0% precision.
