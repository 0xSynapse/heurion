3. ABSTRACT (Single Continuous Paragraph, NO Newlines, NO Extra Whitespace, NO LaTeX Commands):
Autonomous large language model (LLM) agents have achieved remarkable success in software engineering via free-form shell execution and direct file manipulation. However, transplanting this script-and-audit paradigm to high-stakes scientific and clinical research introduces severe failure modes: destructive formatting drift in Office Open XML (.docx and .pptx) manuscripts, silent overwriting of concurrent human edits, fabricated bibliographic identifiers (DOIs and PMIDs) or ungrounded statistical assertions, exposure of Protected Health Information (PHI), and repetitive guard failures across authoring sessions. We present Heurion 2.0, a collaborative clinical research and scientific authoring platform built upon a Contract-Guarded Dual-Surface Architecture and a Three-Layer Self-Evolving Memory System. First, rather than allowing agents to mutate raw document files via ad-hoc scripts, Heurion 2.0 maintains a single Conflict-free Replicated Data Type (CRDT) source of truth (ProseMirror + Yjs) with lifecycle-stable block addressing ({#id}). It exposes a synchronized dual surface: a rich-text and slide editor for human researchers and a compact Annotated Markdown projection with guarded structural primitives for AI agents, supporting per-turn selective undo in CRDT space and 100% byte-preserving OOXML patch export for unmodified regions. Second, to achieve zero-hallucination evidence grounding, pre-write syntactic guards block unregistered inline citations while a multi-agent verification pipeline aligns manuscript sentences against PubMed abstracts and PubMed Central (PMC) full texts. Third, a privacy-preserving clinical research pipeline enforces dual-stage PHI redaction, pseudonymous subject mapping (P001), read-only Personal Health Record (PHR) red-lines, and formally verified Human-AI Permission Symmetry. Finally, a domain-agnostic three-layer self-evolution mechanism combines side-effect-free pre-flight probes (doc_validate), a dual-track memory engine featuring self-supervised corruption-and-reconstruction skill training (Track A) alongside zero-template trajectory-contrastive procedural rule synthesis and backward credit assignment (Track B), and background meta-strategy distillation. Empirical evaluation across deterministic micro-benchmarks and live deepseek-flash multi-session trials demonstrates 100% byte identity on unmodified OOXML blocks, 100% interception of concurrent edit conflicts and unregistered citations at sub-millisecond latency (0.168 ms validation, 0.351 ms edit), a 62.5% overall and 75.0% held-out reduction in guard rejections without hand-crafted rule templates, and zero-negative-transfer cross-domain skill generalization across clinical manuscripts (+155% alignment), biostatistical modeling (0.000 to 0.612), and presentation deck engineering (+161% alignment).
